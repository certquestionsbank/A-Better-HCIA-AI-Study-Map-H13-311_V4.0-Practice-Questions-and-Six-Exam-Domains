## Exam introduction

Huawei calls the certification **HCIA-AI V4.0**. Its official V4.0 exam outline identifies the exam as **H13-311**. The longer identifier **H13-311_V4.0** is useful in a study library because it distinguishes the version, but it is not a separate code printed in the official outline.

This is an associate-level AI syllabus. Candidates need a working grasp of the terms, methods, frameworks, and application contexts named in the guide. The outline is a general guide, and Huawei notes that the test may include content it does not list explicitly. Use the guide to organize review without assuming its short topic labels specify every possible question.

## Official exam topics covered

### AI Overview, 10%

The first domain includes an AI description, major technologies, applications, debates about AI, and an overview of DeepSeek's influence on AI development. Start by explaining each concept in ordinary language. If you cannot distinguish a technology from an application, a multiple-choice item can make familiar words look interchangeable.

### Machine Learning Overview, 20%

This domain covers algorithms, classification of methods, the machine learning process, other important methods, and common algorithms. The local practice material includes questions that distinguish supervised from unsupervised learning and compare algorithm families. Such questions are useful only when you can identify the data and task in the scenario. For example, unlabeled customer records suggest a different first approach from labeled examples of a known outcome.

### Basics of Deep Learning and Foundation Models, 30%

Huawei gives this the largest published weight. Its listed topics are the perceptron, training a fully connected neural network, convolutional networks, recurrent-network architectures, Transformer architecture, and foundation-model infrastructure. The local question set contains many conventional neural-network questions. Use those to test the basics, then review the Transformer and foundation-model topics directly from current V4.0 learning material. Do not assume that older neural-network questions cover the whole domain.

### AI Development Framework, 20%

The guide names AI frameworks, framework basics, PyTorch, and the AI application development process. A useful distinction is between a framework for building and training models and a library that handles a narrower task such as image processing. The local material includes a framework-versus-library comparison. Read that kind of question as a classification exercise, then confirm current product and framework details from their own documentation.

### AI Business Process Overview, 15%

The official topics are the AI business process, the large-model business process, use of large models, and prompt engineering. Build a simple workflow on paper: define a need, identify data, choose an approach, evaluate the output, and decide how people will use it. That is a study exercise rather than a quoted Huawei process. The local bank is thin on prompt-related wording, so use the official V4.0 materials to fill this part of the outline.

### Cutting-edge AI Applications, 5%

Huawei lists voice assistants, smart homes, intelligent vehicles, recommendation, robots, and AI4Science. The goal is to identify the kind of AI problem in an application and the constraints it raises. A voice assistant and a recommendation service can both use models, but their input, output, and user expectations differ.

## Practice checks grounded in the question material

The following are **adapted study checks**, not reproduced Huawei exam items. They use concepts present in the local practice set and align with the V4.0 outline.

**Check 1.** A company has customer records without outcome labels and wants to discover useful groups. Which method family should it examine first? **Answer:** unsupervised learning, such as clustering. **Why:** the requested output is a grouping inferred from unlabeled data. A supervised classifier would need labeled examples of the target outcome.

**Check 2.** Can a single-layer perceptron represent the XOR decision boundary? **Answer:** no. **Why:** a single linear decision boundary cannot separate the XOR classes. This check belongs to the perceptron topic; it does not by itself cover modern foundation models.

**Check 3.** Which kind of component is PyTorch in an AI development workflow? **Answer:** a machine learning development framework. **Why:** the guide names it under AI Development Framework. Avoid confusing a framework with a narrower image-processing library or a deployed application.

## Preparation tips

First, read the Huawei HCIA-AI V4.0 Exam Outline and make a six-row study checklist. Keep the published percentages visible, but do not convert them into a guessed count of exam questions. For each row, write one example you can explain without looking at an answer key.

Second, split the deep-learning domain into older architecture basics and V4.0-specific model topics. Practice perceptrons, fully connected networks, convolutional networks, and recurrent networks with explained questions. Review Transformers and foundation-model infrastructure from the official training material because they need separate attention.

Third, use practice results to identify a **reasoning gap**, not just an incorrect letter. Record the clue in the question, the rule or concept you applied, and why another answer was tempting. If a question appears to test a topic absent from the V4.0 outline, keep it as optional background rather than promoting it to an official objective.

Fourth, review the AI application examples. For each, write down the input, intended output, and one technical or business constraint. That simple comparison makes short application labels more useful.
